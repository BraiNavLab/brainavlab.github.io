We are proud to announce that Dr. Nicola Valè has been selected for a research fellowship funded by the Italian Multiple Sclerosis Foundation (FISM). This recognition reflects the quality and impact of his work in the field of multiple sclerosis. His research continues to contribute to both our laboratory’s activities and the wider scientific community. We warmly congratulate Dr. Valè and look forward to his future achievements.
