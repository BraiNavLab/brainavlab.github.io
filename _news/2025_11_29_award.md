BraiNavLab postdoctoral researcher Nicola Valè received an award in the “Digital Technologies” session at the AIFI conference “Verso l’appropriatezza terapeutica: Moving Between Clinical Practice and Evidence”. The recognition highlights our work on motor–cognitive interaction in multiple sclerosis, presented through a wearable EEG–IMU framework that enables the simultaneous recording of brain activity and movement during dynamic tasks like walking, supported by AI-based processing for automated trial segmentation and analysis.
