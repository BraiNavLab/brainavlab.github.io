description: |
  While machine/deep learning approaches have undeniable advantages, the more complex the model, the more mandatory it is to have explanations to build trust in the outcomes and interpret the results, especially in medicine, healthcare and neuroscience fields. Such complexity leads to questions of trust, bias, and interpretability as machine/deep learning methods are often a “black box”. XAI was born to make the model behaviour comprehensible to humans, aiming at explaining how the model reached a specific outcome, how the features contributed, and to what extent the model is confident about the decision (uncertainty).
